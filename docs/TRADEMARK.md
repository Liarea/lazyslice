# Trademark policy for the name lazyslice

The code is licensed under Apache-2.0, which grants no trademark rights (§6). This policy says what the name may be used for. It is modelled on the Linux Foundation's trademark usage guidelines and exists so that "you may host the code; you may not call your hosted version lazyslice" is written down rather than implied.

## You may, without asking

- Use "lazyslice" to refer to this project, accurately, in articles, talks, package listings, and documentation.
- Distribute unmodified builds of this project under the name lazyslice, including through package managers.
- Say that your product or service "works with lazyslice" or "is built on lazyslice".

## You may not, without written permission from the copyright holder

- Use "lazyslice", or a name confusingly similar to it, as the name of a modified version, a fork, a hosted service, or a commercial product.
- Use the name in a way that suggests the project endorses your product or that your product is the official one.
- Register domains, package names, or accounts whose principal element is "lazyslice" for anything other than pointing at this project.

## Modified versions

A modified version must be renamed. "Based on lazyslice" or "a fork of lazyslice" is fine as a description; "lazyslice Pro" or "lazyslice Cloud" is not.
